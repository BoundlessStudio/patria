Exploring with Patria

Updated November 6, 2026.

Patria helps you discover nearby places and plan neighborhood walks.

Place information and walks

Check a venue’s current details before visiting. Distances in Patria are approximate straight-line distances. Suggested walks show a sequence of stops. Use the linked Google Maps walking directions for an actual route.

Map data and imagery

Google Maps provides the map, live traffic, and Street View. Use is subject to the Google Maps and Google Earth Additional Terms of Service. Location search and nearby place discovery use data from OpenStreetMap contributors under the Open Database License.

Marble scenes

Published scenes are AI-generated interpretations. Use real-world maps and directions for navigation.

Accounts and preferences

Authentication is supplied by Sites. Keep your account access secure. Saved places and preferences are local to this browser. The illustrated home-page scenes are artwork and do not represent a specific location.

Credits

Neighborhood photography is credited under “Photo credits” on the home page. The hero and footer illustrations were created for Patria using image generation.
