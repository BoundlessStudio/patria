Your exploration and privacy

Updated November 6, 2026.

Signing in

Sites handles authentication. Patria uses the verified user identifier and display name supplied by the platform to personalize the experience. The app does not receive your password.

Maps and location search

Google Maps receives the visible map area and the location you request for Street View. Patria sends search text and selected coordinates to Photon to find locations and nearby places using OpenStreetMap data. If you choose “near me,” your browser asks for location permission first.

Saved places and preferences

Saved places and onboarding preferences, including your chosen starting point, stay in this browser’s local storage. Older Google saves store a place ID and link to Google Maps. These preferences do not synchronize across devices. You can remove saved places using their bookmark controls, change preferences through onboarding, or clear this site’s browser storage.

Google services

The Google map, satellite layer, live traffic, Street View, and Google walking directions are subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Marble scenes

Opening a published scene takes you to World Labs. Patria creates those scenes from reviewed reference material and publishes links for visitors to open.

Site services

Sites provides hosting and authentication. Google Fonts supplies the site’s typefaces. The app does not add analytics, advertising trackers, or an email signup form.
