Map sources

Patria uses OpenStreetMap data for streets, buildings, and nearby places. OpenFreeMap serves the map and Photon handles place search. The map loads without an API key.

Sign in on the hosted site to search and discover nearby places. Walk previews follow foot routes from FOSSGIS. Satellite imagery and external walking directions open in Google Maps.

Street-level walks draw stylized bike racks, bins, and transit stop poles from mapped OpenStreetMap features. Sidewalk and curb styling takes cues from Chicken4War’s Kensington Avenue photograph (CC BY-SA 4.0). Sidewalk widths are illustrative.

Animated traffic

At close zoom, Patria simulates cars, streetcars, trains, and ferries along mapped OpenStreetMap routes. Car density uses published average daily volumes on matched roads and road-class estimates elsewhere. Train and ferry timing is illustrative.
